2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
2 tbsp granulated sugar
1 tbsp baking powder
½ tsp salt
2 large eggs, separated
1¾ cups (420ml) milk (or plant-based milk for a vegan option)
½ cup (115g) unsalted butter, melted (or vegetable oil)
1 tsp vanilla extract
Optional toppings: fresh berries, maple syrup, whipped cream, chocolate chips, or powdered sugar
Instructions
Preheat your waffle iron to medium-high heat. Lightly grease it with butter or oil if needed.

Mix dry ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.

Whisk wet ingredients: In another bowl, beat the egg yolks, then add the milk, melted butter (or oil), and vanilla extract. Mix well.

Combine wet and dry ingredients: Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix—a few lumps are fine.

Beat egg whites: In a separate bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form. Gently fold them into the batter for fluffier waffles.

Cook the waffles: Pour about ½ cup of batter onto the preheated waffle iron (adjust based on your iron’s size). Close the lid and cook for 3–5 minutes, or until golden brown and crisp.

Serve warm: Top with your favorite toppings like maple syrup, fresh fruit, whipped cream, or nuts.

Tip: For extra crispiness, place cooked waffles in a warm oven (95°C/200°F) while you finish the batter.
